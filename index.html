The work

A hands-on practice for how you're built and how you move

Rolfing® Structural Integration is bodywork that reorganizes fascia, the connective tissue that wraps every muscle, bone, and organ. Instead of chasing symptoms, it looks at how your whole body is organized in gravity, then works on the holding patterns that keep pulling you back into pain.

Relief from chronic pain

Pain in your back, neck, hip, or shoulder that keeps coming back usually starts somewhere else. We look for where.

Posture that holds

Standing upright stops taking effort. That's different from forcing your shoulders back and hoping it sticks.

Freer, fuller movement

More range and less effort, whether you're coming back from an injury or just want to keep doing what you do.

A new sense of your body

Sessions include movement work, so the changes hold after you leave the table.

What it feels like

Deep work doesn't have to mean bracing through it

Rolfing has a reputation for being intense. The pressure actually varies a lot, sometimes light and listening, sometimes firm. What decides it is what your tissue is willing to change, not how much you can put up with.

You stay involved the whole time. Beth will ask you to breathe into a spot, or to move while she works, and to say what you're feeling. Sessions happen on a table in clothes you can move in, and there's always time to talk about what you notice.

Does Rolfing hurt?

It can be intense in places, but it shouldn't be something you grit your teeth through. Beth adjusts as she goes, based on what you tell her. Work that makes you brace doesn't produce lasting change, so there's no reason to push past what you can stay relaxed through.

How is this different from massage?

Massage generally aims at relaxation and relief in the moment. Rolfing aims at structure: how your body is organized in gravity, and how that organization creates or resolves your symptoms. Sessions follow a plan and build on each other, so the changes hold rather than fade by the weekend.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends what you're bringing in. A specific, recent complaint can often be handled as fix-it work in one to three sessions. Longer-standing patterns usually want a series of three, five, or the full ten. You don't have to decide up front. Come once and you'll both know a lot more.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing you can move in, like running shorts and a sports bra, or fitted athletic wear. Part of the session happens standing and walking, so Beth needs to be able to see how you move. Wear what you'd feel at ease in.
